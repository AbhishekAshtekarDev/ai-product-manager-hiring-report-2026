# AI Product Manager Job Postings 2026: Open Dataset

[![License: CC BY 4.0](https://img.shields.io/badge/License-CC%20BY%204.0-lightgrey.svg)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)
[![Report](https://img.shields.io/badge/Read-the%20full%20report-2463f5.svg)](https://theaiproductmanagerblueprint.com/ai-product-manager-hiring-report-2026/)

The aggregated data behind the **[AI Product Manager Hiring Report 2026](https://theaiproductmanagerblueprint.com/ai-product-manager-hiring-report-2026/)**: 2,448 unique AI product manager job postings from 1,560 companies in 53 countries, captured on 12 September 2026.

Every table the report charts is here as a clean CSV. It covers what employers ask for and what they pay. Skills and salaries sit next to degree and experience asks, with a table for every location cut and role type in the report. Use it in articles, research, course material or your own analysis. The only condition is a credit with a link to the report.

## Ten findings in one minute

1. **84% name no programming language.** Python shows up in 9% of postings and SQL in 11%. See [how technical AI PM roles are](https://theaiproductmanagerblueprint.com/ai-product-manager-hiring-report-2026/#ch-technical).
2. **22% make a degree a hard requirement.** 53% never mention education. Another 11% accept equivalent experience. See [the degree data](https://theaiproductmanagerblueprint.com/ai-product-manager-hiring-report-2026/#ch-degrees).
3. **5 years is the median experience asked.** Only 8% of postings that state a number accept two years or fewer. See [experience by level](https://theaiproductmanagerblueprint.com/ai-product-manager-hiring-report-2026/#ch-experience).
4. **$193K median posted salary.** The midpoint of 904 posted ranges. The US median is $198K and the top tenth of roles start at $265K. See [the salary chapter](https://theaiproductmanagerblueprint.com/ai-product-manager-hiring-report-2026/#ch-money).
5. **30% of roles are agentic AI PM jobs.** The largest of 8 role types, ahead of GenAI and LLM product roles at 26%. See [the 8 role types](https://theaiproductmanagerblueprint.com/ai-product-manager-hiring-report-2026/#ch-role).
6. **76% ask for roadmapping.** Product craft beats every named AI technique. RAG appears in 19% of postings. See [the full skills ranking](https://theaiproductmanagerblueprint.com/ai-product-manager-hiring-report-2026/#ch-skills).
7. **76% of companies posted a single role.** The ten biggest employers hold just 8% of all postings. See [who is hiring](https://theaiproductmanagerblueprint.com/ai-product-manager-hiring-report-2026/#ch-employers).
8. **$232K median in the $200K+ segment.** Those roles ask for 7 years instead of 5, and they lean harder on go-to-market work and on mentoring. See [what separates $200K+ roles](https://theaiproductmanagerblueprint.com/ai-product-manager-hiring-report-2026/#ch-money).
9. **20% ask for AI evaluation.** When a posting splits must-haves from nice-to-haves, evals land in the must-have block 66% of the time. See [the AI stack data](https://theaiproductmanagerblueprint.com/ai-product-manager-hiring-report-2026/#ch-ai-stack).
10. **13% of postings are fully remote.** Hybrid is 25%. Canada is the most remote-friendly big market at 26%. See [remote and hybrid work](https://theaiproductmanagerblueprint.com/ai-product-manager-hiring-report-2026/#fig-work-mode).

## How the data was built

Postings came straight from the applicant tracking systems employers publish on (Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Workday, SmartRecruiters and others) wherever possible, and from public job platform listings where a company board was not reachable.

1. **Collect.** 8,073 AI-relevant postings.
2. **De-duplicate.** Six matching passes (URL, description within a company, description across sources, requisition ID and two near-duplicate checks) removed 2,875 copies, 36% of everything collected. 5,198 unique postings remained.
3. **Keep real AI PM jobs.** A title filter kept product management roles and dropped product marketing, design, program management, analytics and engineering. Each posting was then tiered by how central AI is to the job. Tier A has AI in the title (1,784). Tier B has a general PM title where AI is the core of the work (664). Together they form the 2,448-posting core dataset.
4. **Measure.** Skills come from a 140-term dictionary that also spots required versus preferred blocks. Degree and experience asks are parsed too, and salary ranges are annualised to US dollars (904 postings disclose pay).

The complete method, with every caveat, is in [chapter 1 of the report](https://theaiproductmanagerblueprint.com/ai-product-manager-hiring-report-2026/#ch-method).

**Read the salary tables carefully.** Posted pay is a base salary range, not total compensation. The UK has 13 postings with pay and India has none, so neither gets a headline pay figure here.
