Ten findings from the data

Each one links to the report chapter with the charts and the method behind it.

  1. 84% name no programming language

    Python shows up in 9% of postings and SQL in 11%. See how technical AI PM roles are.

  2. 22% make a degree a hard requirement

    53% never mention education. Another 11% accept equivalent experience. See the degree data.

  3. 5 years is the median experience asked

    Only 8% of postings that state a number accept two years or fewer. See experience by level.

  4. $193K median posted salary

    The midpoint of 904 posted ranges. The US median is $198K and the top tenth of roles start at $265K. See the salary chapter.

  5. 30% of roles are agentic AI PM jobs

    The largest of 8 role types, ahead of GenAI and LLM product roles at 26%. See the 8 role types.

  6. 76% ask for roadmapping

    Product craft beats every named AI technique. RAG appears in 19% of postings. See the full skills ranking.

  7. 76% of companies posted a single role

    The ten biggest employers hold just 8% of all postings. See who is hiring.

  8. $232K median in the $200K+ segment

    Those roles ask for 7 years instead of 5, and they lean harder on go-to-market work and on mentoring. See what separates $200K+ roles.

  9. 20% ask for AI evaluation

    When a posting splits must-haves from nice-to-haves, evals land in the must-have block 66% of the time. See the AI stack data.

  10. 13% of postings are fully remote

    Hybrid is 25%. Canada is the most remote-friendly big market at 26%. See remote and hybrid work.

How it was built

  1. Collect. 8,073 AI-relevant postings, pulled from employer applicant tracking systems (Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Workday, SmartRecruiters) wherever possible and public job platforms where not.
  2. De-duplicate. Six matching passes removed 2,875 copies, 36% of everything collected.
  3. Keep real AI PM jobs. Product roles only, then tiered by how central AI is. Tier A (1,784) has AI in the title. Tier B (664) makes AI the core of a general PM role.
  4. Measure. Skills come from a 140-term dictionary that also spots required versus preferred blocks. Degree and experience asks are parsed too, and salary ranges are annualised to US dollars.

Posted pay is base salary, not total compensation. The UK has too few postings with pay and India has none, so neither gets a headline figure. The full methodology covers every rule and caveat.
